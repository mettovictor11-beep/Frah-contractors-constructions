# Frah Contractors - Official Construction Website

A modern, high-performance React website engineered for **Frah Contractors**.

Built with **React**, **Vite**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Lucide Icons**, following a clean, authoritative construction design language with the specified color palette: **Navy Blue**, **Primary Theme Blue**, **Crisp White**, and **Slate Greys**.

---

## 🎨 Theme Color Palette

- **Navy Blue (Foundation & Structure):** `#060d1a`, `#0a172e`, `#0e1e3d`, `#142a53`
- **Primary Blue (Accents & Highlights):** `#0e87ea`, `#026bc7`, `#38a5f7`
- **White (Backgrounds & Cards):** `#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`
- **Grey (Subtext & Borders):** `#64748b`, `#94a3b8`, `#e2e8f0`

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Top Emergency & Credentials Bar**: 24/7 hotline, operating hours, and Class A contractor license status.
2. **Sticky Navigation Bar**: Smooth scrolling links, quick call button, and "Request a Quote" CTA.
3. **Hero Section**: High-impact headline, trust markers, live construction site preview card, and instant action buttons.
4. **Stats Counter**: Completed projects, years in business, safety rating, and client satisfaction.
5. **Interactive Services Showcase**: 6 core contracting sectors (Commercial, Residential, Civil & Infrastructure, Remodeling, Industrial & Warehousing, Design-Build) with tabs and feature checklists.
6. **Company Standards & Why Us**: 6 pillars highlighting safety, on-time delivery, bonding, and dedicated project management.
7. **Filterable Projects Portfolio**: Category filters (Commercial, Residential, Civil, Industrial, Renovation) with interactive project detail modal.
8. **Construction Process**: 4-phase structured lifecycle from feasibility to handover and warranty.
9. **Interactive Cost Estimator Widget**: Calculates real-time ballpark estimates based on project type, square footage, finish tier, and timeline, with direct tender submission!
10. **Testimonials & FAQ Accordion**: Verified client reviews and frequently asked questions about permits, bonding, and change orders.
11. **Contact & Bid Request Form**: Full form with validation, success feedback, and office coordinates.
12. **Quote Modal**: Quick pop-up consultation request accessible from anywhere on the page.
